This week at a glance

  • How things are going. The spas brought 142 leads into their CRMs this week at $28.08 each, compared with 204 at $24.32 the week before, on lower spend of $3,987.04 against $4,960.35. The drop likely follows the end of Bellatox KY's open house campaign. Desert Zen led with 34 leads at $10.21 each.
  • What to look into. Meta counted 238 leads but 142 reached the CRMs. Sanctuaire's 48 Meta leads cannot be checked because its CRM is not connected yet. Among the connected spas the biggest gaps are Bellatox KY (47 against 32), Bellatox IN (40 against 26) and Essential Health (17 against 9).
  • What is next. Desert Zen is where added budget is likely to go furthest: its leads cost $10.21 each, the lowest in the group. We recommend raising its tummy rescue budget in small steps, and keeping each increase only while the cost per lead stays near that level.
  • Creative ideas to consider. Plain, specific offers won. Desert Zen's new text image, 'The T-Shape 2 has arrived at Desert Zen,' first tummy rescue session $149 instead of $399, brought 27 leads at $8.20 each. Sanctuaire's video, 'accepting 15 tox and filler models this month,' tox at $9.99 a unit, brought 21 at $8.75. Bellatox IN's new ad, '20 units of tox for $179,' brought 13 at $10.25. We recommend testing the text-image format for McKinney's cryo launch, a similar offer that spent $239.63 for 3 leads.
